Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

Arcade Collection doesn't have accounts, doesn't collect personal information, and doesn't require you to sign up for anything. Each game saves your own high scores and progress locally in your browser (via localStorage) — that data stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalization by visiting www.aboutads.info.

For more detail on how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps, see How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

What we don't do

  • We don't require or ask for your name, email, or any account registration.
  • We don't sell or share any personal data, because we don't collect any in the first place.
  • We don't use the scores or progress saved in your browser for anything other than displaying them back to you on that same device.

Cookies and local storage

Aside from any cookies set by advertising partners described above, this site uses your browser's localStorage only to remember things like high scores, best times, and mute preferences for each game. This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers — we don't operate a server that collects it.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us using the details below and we'll address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example as advertising partners or features change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
